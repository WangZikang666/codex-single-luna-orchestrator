# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- giving and receiving technical feedback in good faith;
- respecting privacy and avoiding publication of credentials or private data;
- helping keep discussions relevant and useful.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For sensitive conduct concerns, avoid posting private information in a public issue. Use a
private contact method available through GitHub where possible.

This project aims to apply these standards consistently and proportionately.
